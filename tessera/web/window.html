Unlock with your iPhone

Pair your iPhone once, then unlock this laptop by running a shortcut and looking at your phone. Your password keeps working as before.

Paired iPhones

    Unlocking

    • Run the Tessera shortcut
      From the Home Screen, or double-tap the back of your iPhone with Back Tap.
    • Your password
      Still works, as always.

    Pair an iPhone

    1. Scan this with your iPhone's camera
      It shows how to make the two shortcuts. Only needed the first time.
    2. Run the Tessera shortcut
      Save the passkey with Face ID, then tap Send to Laptop.
    Waiting for your iPhone

    Check the Code

    Your iPhone shows a four-digit code. It should be the same as this one.

    If they differ, another device answered at the same time. Choose Cancel and pair again.
